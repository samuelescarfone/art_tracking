# Sterling Crispin

## Summary
Sterling Crispin (b. 1985, Maui, Hawaii) is a conceptual artist and software engineer working with smart contracts, generative art, machine intelligence and "techno-sculpture" [1]. His Denver ties date mostly from 2008–2011: he studied at RMCAD, was a RedLine Artist in Residence (2008–2011), and showed in a 2010 group show at Robischon Gallery [1][2][3]. He later showed in MCA Denver's 2015 Biennial of the Americas exhibition [1]. He is now a mid-career digital and on-chain artist with institutional group-show credits (ZKM, KW Berlin, Kunsthal Charlottenborg, Seoul Mediacity Biennale) [1], alongside a substantial tech career, including work as a former Apple "Neurotechnology Prototyping Researcher" on Apple Vision Pro development [4]. His current location is unclear: Artsy says California, False Flag lists both New Mexico and California, and his X profile says "united states" [5][6][7].

## Practice & Style
- Conceptual work across smart contracts, generative art, machine intelligence and techno-sculpture [1]. Instagram bio: "Art + code On chain art and smart contracts" [8].
- Art Blocks projects include *Flourish* and *Neophyte MMXXII* [9].
- Recent projects listed on his site: "Stillpoint / ModelWelfare.xyz" (2026), "Op Codes" (2026), "Like a Ship Without A Sail" (2024), and "Del Complex" (2023–24, which included an "AI training barge" concept covered by VICE) [1]. No venues or dates were found for these.
- Graduate theses linked on his site: an MS thesis ("Data-Masks") and an MFA thesis ("CHARON"). The granting school(s) were not confirmed [1].
- His public focus has shifted toward AI and tech. His X bio reads "Artist + Software Developer / Applied AI / Agent Systems / Autonomous Trading" [7]. He is a co-inventor on AR headset patents [10]. Variety covered Apple hiring him in July 2018 (per his CV) [1].
- He also has a music profile under the alias "DJ Mediafire" [11].
