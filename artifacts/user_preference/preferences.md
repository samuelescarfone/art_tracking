# My Collecting Preferences

The curator reads this file before deciding whether an artist goes on the tracking list.
Edit it any time. When it changes, artists are re-checked against the new version as they come up.

## Do NOT track
- Digital art: NFTs, generative/code-based work, AI art, video, new media, screen-based work
- Statues and sculpture: three-dimensional figurative or object-based work
- Abstract art: non-representational painting or work on paper
- Contemporary/conceptual art: installation, conceptual, performance and experimental practices

## Notes
- If an artist works in several modes, judge by their main body of work.
- If there isn't enough information to tell, reject and say so in the reason.
